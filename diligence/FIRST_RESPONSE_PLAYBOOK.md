# Qualified inquiry response playbook

Reply manually from `sales@avarent.app`. Target the same business day when practical. Do not add the contact to a newsletter or automated sequence.

## First reply

Subject: Re: Avarent evaluation question

Thank you for the concrete question. Based on what you sent, the first review can stay outside production.

We propose evaluating:

- Question: [repeat their question]
- Starting input: [synthetic, de-identified, or no data for document review]
- Method: [one-sentence method]
- Output: [specific artifact]
- Known limitation: [most important uncertainty]
- Next decision: [what both sides decide after inspection]

No payment or production access is needed to review this boundary. If this matches your intent, please send the roles that need to review it and two or three suitable meeting times.

Regards,
[George or Lucas]

## Follow-up discipline

- One useful follow-up after three business days, including the sample packet or relevant diligence section.
- One final close-the-loop note after seven business days.
- Stop after a decline, unsubscribe request, or clear lack of fit.
- Track institution, contact, evaluation question, last action, next action, and outcome. Do not store sensitive applicant information.
