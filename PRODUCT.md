# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Credit-union, community-bank, bank, and lending-fintech leaders evaluating AI or algorithmic credit decisioning. Primary evaluators are fair-lending and compliance officers, model-risk teams, security reviewers, IT/procurement staff, and product or risk leaders. They arrive skeptical and need to understand Avarent's scope, evidence, limitations, and pilot risk before agreeing to a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Avarent helps lending teams inspect credit-decision outcomes for disparity and explanation risk, investigate findings, and assemble reviewable evidence. The website's job is to earn a qualified pilot conversation by making the evaluation path concrete and low risk, not by implying certification or guaranteed compliance.

## Brand Personality

Measured, technical, candid. The desired feeling is calm scrutiny: Avarent expects hard questions and answers them directly.

## Anti-references

No cybersecurity cosplay, neon AI imagery, anonymous mega-corporation voice, invented client logos, inflated performance statistics, vague “enterprise-grade” claims, compliance guarantees, endless identical card grids, or founder-story hero worship.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence before assertion.
2. Make the low-risk evaluation path visible on every high-intent page.
3. State limitations and unearned credentials plainly.
4. Separate illustrative product examples from customer or production results.
5. Give each evaluator the exact next document or answer they need.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard operability, visible focus, reduced-motion support, semantic landmarks, readable line lengths, and layouts that work without horizontal scrolling at 320, 375, 414, and 768 pixels.
