# Avarent Synthetic Fair-Lending Evidence Review

## The offer

A fixed-scope review for a lender that wants to evaluate one algorithmic credit-decision question without replacing its model or beginning with a production integration.

The buyer is purchasing an inspectable analysis and evidence package, not a compliance certification, legal opinion, or automated credit-decision system.

## Recommended commercial terms

- **Scoping conversation:** no charge, 20–30 minutes, no data requested.
- **Design-partner review:** **$2,500 fixed**.
- **Payment structure:** 50% when the written scope is accepted, 50% when the evidence packet and readout are delivered.
- **Delivery target:** 10 business days after Avarent accepts the agreed dataset and data dictionary.
- **Change control:** a second workflow, materially different dataset, or expanded analysis requires a new written scope.

Do not discount by default. If a strategically important institution cannot approve $2,500, reduce scope before reducing price. Never condition a discount on a public logo or testimonial; any public reference requires separate written permission.

## One question, one workflow

The review begins with one concrete question, for example:

- Are favorable-outcome rates materially different across the agreed cohorts?
- Can an identified disparity be documented with enough context for qualified review?
- Are adverse-action reasons specific, traceable, and reviewable against the available decision fields?
- Can the institution reproduce the agreed calculation and understand its limitations?

## Inputs

The final field list is agreed in writing. The default starting point is:

- one synthetic or institution-approved de-identified tabular extract;
- one decision workflow and outcome definition;
- an agreed reference group and comparison cohorts;
- one analysis window;
- a data dictionary;
- existing decision reasons or relevant decision fields, when explanation review is in scope.

The initial review does not require credentials, direct production access, applicant documents, free-text applications, or a live model integration.

## Deliverables

1. **Scope and configuration note** recording the question, population, outcome, groups, time window, thresholds, assumptions, and exclusions.
2. **Reproducible disparity screen** using the agreed measures and configuration.
3. **Finding record** pairing every reported result with comparison context, limitations, and the next human-review action.
4. **Decision-reason review** when included in scope, clearly separating candidate explanations from institution-approved adverse-action reasons.
5. **Evidence packet** containing methods, findings, reviewer prompts, limitations, and artifact/version context.
6. **45-minute readout** for compliance, model-risk, or product stakeholders.
7. **Export and exit** providing the agreed artifacts and documenting deletion or retention expectations for the review data.

## Success criteria

Before work begins, the parties choose two or three observable criteria. Recommended criteria are:

- Avarent reproduces one agreed baseline measure within an agreed tolerance.
- A reviewer can trace a finding to its population, comparison, window, and configuration.
- The exported packet contains enough context for a qualified reviewer to challenge the result.
- Known limitations and missing information are visible rather than silently resolved by the software.
- The institution can identify whether a limited next-stage pilot is justified.

“Become compliant,” “eliminate bias,” and “receive regulatory approval” are not valid success criteria.

## Explicit exclusions

- legal advice or a legal determination;
- regulatory approval or certification;
- a guarantee that a model, policy, or institution is compliant;
- production deployment;
- model replacement or automated underwriting;
- causal conclusions from disparity measurements alone;
- public use of the institution’s name, logo, or results.

## Qualification rules

Proceed only when the prospect has:

- a real lending workflow or evaluation question;
- a named compliance, model-risk, risk, or product owner;
- the ability to provide a usable synthetic or approved de-identified extract;
- willingness to define success and limitations in writing;
- a credible path to a broader pilot if the review is useful.

Decline or defer when the prospect wants a compliance guarantee, asks Avarent to make credit decisions, cannot name an internal reviewer, or expects sensitive data transfer before scope and controls are agreed.

## Sales language

**One sentence:** Avarent runs a fixed-scope, synthetic-first review that turns one fair-lending question into a reproducible finding and an inspectable evidence packet.

**Call to action:** Bring one evaluation question. Avarent will return a proposed method, minimum inputs, deliverables, limitations, and a clear next decision before requesting data or payment.
